# Data Cleaning Exercise: Historical Dataset

In this exercise, you will practice data cleaning skills using a historical dataset. The goal is to take a messy dataset and transform it into a clean, standardized format that can be used for analysis.

You will access a messy historical dataset (e.g., census records, voter registration, or similar) and use pandas to:

- Load and explore the data
- Identify data quality issues (missing values, inconsistent formats, duplicates)
- Clean and standardize the dataset
- Document your cleaning decisions and what was lost/transformed

You can use any historical dataset you find interesting, or you can use the [kaggle dataset about UFO sightings](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/NUFORC/ufo-sightings)

The exercise is open-ended, so feel free to be creative in your approach. The key is to demonstrate your ability to clean and prepare data for analysis.

The assignment is due by 9 am Feb. 9 2026. You will submit: 

- a Jupyter notebook with your code and explanations of your cleaning process.
- an AI log that documents your interactions with AI tools during the cleaning process, including any prompts you used and the responses you received. It's ok to use AI tools to assist with data cleaning, but you must document your interactions and the decisions you made based on the AI's suggestions.
- the original messy dataset you used for the exercise (if it's not too large to upload)
- any resulting cleaned dataset(s)
